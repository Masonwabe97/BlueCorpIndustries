Resident Evil Village

Released by Capcom in 2021, Resident Evil Village is the eighth major story entry in the survival horror series. Players control Ethan Winters as he navigates a snowy, monster-infested European settlement to rescue his kidnapped infant daughter, Rose, after a violent intrusion by series veteran Chris Redfield.

Set a few years after Resident Evil 7: Biohazard, it follows Ethan Winters as he navigates a snowy, myth-laden European village to rescue his kidnapped infant daughter from four eccentric mutant lords and their supreme leader, Mother Miranda.

Specifications

To run Resident Evil Village on PC, you need a minimum of an Intel Core i5-7500 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 CPU, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1050 Ti or AMD Radeon RX 560 with 4GB of VRAM, targeting 1080p/60fps with performance priorities.

  • Developer: Capcom
  • Publisher: Capcom
  • Release Date: October 1, 2021
  • Genre: Survival Horror, Action

God of War IV

God of War IV is an action-adventure game developed by Santa Monica Studio and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. The game follows Kratos and his son Atreus as they journey through the Norse realms.

An older Kratos tries to live a quiet life and mentors his young son, Atreus. Together, they embark on a deeply emotional quest to scatter the ashes of Kratos's late wife at the highest peak in the realms.

Specifications

To run God of War IV on PC, you need a minimum of an Intel Core i5-6600K or AMD Ryzen 5 1400 CPU, 8 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 970 or AMD Radeon R9 290 with 4GB of VRAM, targeting 1080p/60fps with performance priorities.

  • Developer: Santa Monica Studio

  • Release Date: April 20, 2018

Peak

Peak is a puzzle game developed by Blue Corp Industries. The game challenges players to solve increasingly complex puzzles by manipulating the environment and using logic and strategy.

The game features a minimalist art style and a relaxing soundtrack, providing a calming experience for players.

Specifications

To run Peak on PC, you need a minimum of an Intel Core i3-6100 or AMD Ryzen 3 1200 CPU, 4 GB of RAM, and an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 750 or AMD Radeon R7 250 with 2GB of VRAM, targeting 1080p/60fps with performance priorities.

  • Developer: Team PEAK (Aggro Crab & Landfall Games)
